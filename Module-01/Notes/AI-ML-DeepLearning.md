# Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Deep Learning

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of Computer Science that focuses on developing machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, and understanding language.

**Examples:**
- ChatGPT and virtual assistants
- Self-driving cars
- Facial recognition
- Smart home systems

## 2. Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Instead of following fixed instructions, ML algorithms identify patterns in data and use them to make predictions or decisions.

**Types of Machine Learning:**
- **Supervised Learning:** Learns from labelled data.
- **Unsupervised Learning:** Finds patterns in unlabelled data.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** Learns through rewards and penalties.

**Examples:**
- Email spam detection
- House price prediction
- Customer segmentation
- Recommendation systems

## 3. Deep Learning (DL)

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

It is particularly useful for processing images, audio, video, and natural language.

**Examples:**
- Face recognition
- Speech recognition
- Language translation
- Autonomous vehicles
- Generative AI

## 4. Difference Between AI, ML and DL

| Feature | AI | ML | DL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Artificial Intelligence | Machine Learning | Deep Learning |
| Scope | Broadest field | Subset of AI | Subset of ML |
| Approach | Rules and learning | Learns from data | Neural networks |
| Data | Varies | Usually requires data | Often requires large datasets |
| Complexity | Varies | Moderate | Generally higher |

## 5. Relationship Between AI, ML and DL

- AI is the broader concept of making machines intelligent.
- ML is an approach that allows machines to learn from data.
- DL is an advanced ML approach based on multi-layer neural networks.
