# Real-World Applications of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is used in various industries to automate tasks, analyze data, and solve real-world problems.

## 1. Healthcare
- Disease detection using medical images.
- AI-assisted diagnosis.
- Drug discovery.

## 2. Finance
- Fraud detection.
- Credit risk assessment.
- Algorithmic trading.

## 3. Education
- Personalized learning.
- AI-powered tutoring systems.
- Automated grading.

## 4. Transportation
- Self-driving vehicles.
- Traffic prediction.
- Route optimization.

## 5. E-commerce
- Product recommendations.
- Customer behavior analysis.
- Chatbots and virtual assistants.

## 6. Agriculture
- Crop disease detection.
- Smart irrigation.
- Yield prediction.

## Conclusion
AI is transforming different industries by improving efficiency, automating processes, and supporting better decision-making.
